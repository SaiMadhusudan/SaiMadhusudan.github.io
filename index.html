01 — About

A researcher who ships.

I'm a Computer Science researcher at IIIT Hyderabad, working at the intersection of computer vision, NLP, and multimodal learning. My research focuses on document intelligence — teaching models to read, parse, and reason over complex documents from modern PDFs to centuries-old palm-leaf manuscripts.

Right now I'm a Generative AI Research Intern at BharatGen (India's National AI Mission), where I lead work on Patram-7B-Instruct — a multilingual vision-language model for Indian languages. I've trained it on 64 H100 GPUs using DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, and built the data pipelines that fuel it.

I've published at top venues including CVPR 2026, WACV 2026, and ICDAR 2026, with two more under review at ECCV. I care about research that's both rigorous and useful — models that work on real, messy, low-resource data.
