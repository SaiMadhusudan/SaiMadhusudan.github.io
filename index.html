Hey! I'm Sai, an M.S. by Research student at IIIT Hyderabad with 3+ years of experience building production ML systems. I've led M3Grounder (CVPR 2026), a document grounding framework for precise evidence localization, and CURIO (WACV 2026), an efficient OCR framework for historical manuscripts. I also worked on UniLipi, an ICDAR paper focused on building a unified multi-script OCR model for historical Indic manuscripts.

Currently, I'm developing Patram-7B-Instruct at BharatGen, a multilingual vision-language model for document understanding. I specialize in production inference optimization, distributed training across 256 H100s, and end-to-end system ownership, with two additional papers accepted at ECCV 2026.

I am advised by Prof. Ravi Kiran Sarvadevabhatla at CVIT.
